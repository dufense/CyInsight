Core Risk Score Formula (High-Level Model)

A strong scoring formula should look like:

Risk Score =
(Threat Intel Weight × TI Score)
+ (Behavior Weight × Behavior Score)
+ (Exposure Weight × Exposure Score)
+ (Vulnerability Weight × Vulnerability Score)
+ (Control Gap Weight × Control Gap Score)
+ (Business Criticality Weight × Impact Score)


Normalize everything to 0–100.

3️⃣ Control-Based Risk Adjustment (Critical)

Your platform should reduce risk if strong controls exist.

Example:

If:

EDR installed & healthy → -10 risk

MFA enforced → -15 risk

Network segmented → -5 risk

Immutable backup present → -10 risk

If missing → add risk.

This makes your model control-aware and maturity-sensitive.

4️⃣ Threat Intel Weighting Strategy

Not all intel signals are equal.

Example weights:

Signal Type	Weight

Known ransomware infrastructure	20
Dark web credential leak	15
OSINT mention	5
Passive DNS anomaly	8

Make weights dynamic based on:

Recency

Source confidence

Correlation count

5️⃣ Recency & Time Decay Model

Risk should decay over time.

Use exponential decay:

Adjusted Score = Base Score × e^(-λ × Days)


Recent activity = higher weight
Old activity = decays gradually

This prevents permanent high-risk scoring.

6️⃣ Correlation Multiplier (Very Important)

If multiple signals align:

Example:

User clicked phishing

Endpoint beaconing

Domain malicious

Credential found on dark web

Add correlation multiplier:

If correlated_signals > 3:
Risk × 1.3


This avoids isolated false positives.
